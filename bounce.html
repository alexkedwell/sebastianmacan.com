What Bounce does

Bounce grabs a slice of your beat and repeats it like a dropped ball: each repeat falls in pitch and volume until it settles. Instant fills, stutters and glitch moments that stay in time with your song.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Bounce on a track. Drums, a full loop, a vocal chop: anything with rhythm works great.
2. Smash the TRIGGER pad. While the music plays, hit TRIGGER. Bounce grabs the moment and starts bouncing it.
3. Pick your division. 1/8 for classic stutters, faster for glitchy rolls, slower for big dramatic bounces.

Make it yours

REPEATS and DECAY. How many bounces you get and how fast they die away. Long decay is a dying echo, short decay is a tight fill.
PITCH FALL. How far the pitch drops with each repeat. Turn it up for that falling-ball sound.
AUTO mode with seeds. Let Bounce fire itself. Seeded, so a happy accident can be recalled and played again, exactly the same, every time.
MIX and OUT. Blend the bounces with your dry signal and set the final level.

Pro tip

Automate TRIGGER right before every drop and transition. One press at the end of bar 8 turns a plain loop into an arrangement.
