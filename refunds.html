Refund Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

The short version

If a product does not work as advertised on your supported system and we cannot fix it for you, you get your money back. We are producers too and we are not interested in keeping money for software you cannot use.

14-day refund window

You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase if:

  • the product fails to install or load on a supported system and format listed on the product page, and
  • you have contacted us at hello@aluetion.com and given us a reasonable chance to resolve the problem.

Most technical issues turn out to be quick fixes (installation path, DAW rescan, format mismatch), so please reach out first. If we cannot get it working, we refund promptly and in full.

What is not covered

  • Buying the wrong format or an unsupported operating system when requirements are clearly listed on the product page. Contact us anyway; we will try to sort something out.
  • "I changed my mind" more than 14 days after purchase.
  • Free products (there is nothing to refund).

How to request a refund

Email hello@aluetion.com with your order number or the email address used at checkout, and a short description of the issue. Refunds are processed by our merchant of record back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

EU and UK customers

Because our products are digital downloads delivered immediately, by completing your purchase you consent to immediate delivery and acknowledge that the statutory 14-day withdrawal right for distance contracts is waived once the download begins, as permitted by applicable consumer law. Our voluntary 14-day policy above still applies regardless.
