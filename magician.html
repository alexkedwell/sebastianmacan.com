What Magician does

Magician writes chord progressions and melodies for you. Pick a genre, smash GENERATE, and you get a fresh progression with a matching melody every single click. When you hear one you love, drag the MIDI straight into your DAW and it is yours.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Magician on a track. In your DAW, add Magician as an instrument on a MIDI track.
2. Pick a genre and hit GENERATE. Every click writes a brand new progression and melody in that style. Keep clicking until something grabs you.
3. Drag the MIDI into your DAW. Grab the chords or the melody right out of Magician and drop them onto any track. Now play them with any sound you want.
