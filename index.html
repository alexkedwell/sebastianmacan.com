Randomizer Multi-FX

CHAOS

One button. Infinite sounds.

A randomizer multi-effect that reinvents your sound on every click — pitch, phaser, flanger, distortion, pan and filter movement, rolled into one hit. Found something insane? The seed number is the sound: 48211937. Save it. Share it. Recall it forever.

  • One-button randomize
  • Seed save & share
  • 29 params, fully automatable
  • Soft-clipped — never blows up

Lo-Fi Synthesizer · Instrument

DUSTY

A synth that remembers cassette tapes.

An 8-voice polysynth soaked in wow, flutter, vinyl crackle and tape saturation. Two oscillators, a warm filter, and a LO-FI section that takes you from gentle bedroom haze to fully chewed tape. Ships with 30 presets — dusty keys, VHS pads, wobbly leads, vaporwave chords.

  • 30 presets included
  • Tape wobble + vinyl crackle
  • Bitcrush & saturation
  • Chorus + spring-style reverb

Granular Texture Machine · Effect

GHOST

Freeze sound. Then haunt it.

Ghost constantly listens, and when you hit FREEZE it traps the last four seconds forever. Drag the XY pad to physically move through the frozen audio — scattering grains, smearing pitch, reversing time. Vocals become choirs. Loops become fog. Anything becomes a texture.

  • Freeze & granular smear
  • XY pad — play inside the audio
  • ±2 octave grain pitch + shimmer
  • Seeded randomize, like Chaos
Download Ghost — Free
v1.1 · macOS · VST3 + AU effect
Shimmer Reverb · Effect

SUGAR

Turn anything into candy.

A shimmer reverb with a sweet tooth. The giant GLOW knob pushes size, octave-up shimmer and sparkle together — from a gentle sugar dusting to a full glassy cathedral. Pads bloom, vocals halo, plucks turn to stardust.

  • GLOW — one giant knob
  • Octave-up shimmer feedback
  • Sparkle exciter + width
  • Soft-clipped — never blows up
Download Sugar — Free
v1.0 · macOS · VST3 + AU effect
Beat Repeater · Effect

BOUNCE

Stutter like a bouncing ball.

A tempo-synced beat repeater that grabs a slice of your audio and bounces it — each repeat can fall in pitch and volume like a dropped ball. Smash the TRIGGER pad by hand, or set AUTO loose with a seed and let it glitch your groove reproducibly.

  • Tempo-synced 1/4 to 1/32
  • Pitch-fall bounce repeats
  • AUTO seeded self-glitching
  • TRIGGER pad performance
